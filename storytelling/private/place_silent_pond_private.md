# Dialectic: GOODNESS IS A LEDGER  
**Revealed Thing:** The belief that some water absorbs merit without returning it.  
**Why it's an if/then:** *If* virtue is transferable, *then* a place that takes but never gives back is a void — a black hole in the moral economy.  

**Episode Hooks:**  
- A boy throws in his father’s belt buckle — the only thing left after the mill fire. It vanishes. He feels lighter.  
- The district officer plans to drain the pond for “sanitation.” Phra Kam does not move.  
- A monk once tried to bless the water. The next day, his name was erased from the temple rolls.  

**Noun Phrase:** *Pond that eats piety*  

**Spatial Spots:**  
- The Lotus Ring (where flowers float, unmoving)  
- The Sunk Spoon  
- The Children’s Diving Stone  
- The Warehouse Threshold (collapsed)  
- The Muddy Edge (where things don’t wash up)
