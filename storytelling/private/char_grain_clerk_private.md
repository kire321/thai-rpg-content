# PRIVATE — The Grain Clerk

**If/then implication of Rangsri's contradiction**  
A vessel who cannot be filled tends the fullness of others — he administers sustenance but takes none, believing consumption would break his neutrality.

**Function**  
The party must verify ancestral claims, retrieve lost tokens, or challenge distribution rulings.

**Hooks**  
- He refuses to accept a donation marked with Rangsri’s scarf pattern — though no name is given, he says the debt is already paid.  
- His beads stopped increasing years ago. He still wears them. Says they weigh less each year.

**Noun phrases**  
empty ledger  
silent ration  
name debt

**Voice notes**  
“Hunger is not the worst thing. The worst thing is being remembered by a mouth that won’t feed you.”  
“I do not eat here. My name is not on any sack.”  
“They think I keep the records. I only guard the silence between names.”
