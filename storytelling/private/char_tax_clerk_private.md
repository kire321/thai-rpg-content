## If/Then Implication of Recurring Character's Contradiction
Pom holds back truth to protect voice; the Tax Clerk erases voice to protect truth.  
*Smaller instance of the same shape:* The man who preserves accuracy by removing emotion believes deeply in fairness—but enforces it by burning what makes it human. His restraint is duty; his grief is that no one thanks him in their own words.

## Recurring Function
The party must obtain or dispute official entries in the ledger—land rights, rations, inheritance claims. Only he can stamp changes, and only after “cooling” any appeal for one full day.
