### If/then implication of the recurring character's contradiction  
Kamnan believes the state is a living horoscope but will not speak its death.  
**Then** the Water Blessing Drummer alters the rhythm—just once—when the king weakens, syncing his breath to the reign’s falter. He cannot speak the truth, but his body can *drift* the time. He embodies the same shape: he feels the sky’s end but must return to the official beat.  

### Recurring function  
The party must confirm the legitimacy of a naming or blessing, which requires the drum to have struck at the correct interval.  

### Hooks  
- Lek notices the drum is off by one minute. The drummer says, “My breath changed. Not the drum.”  
- Kamnan stands at the ghat at dawn. The drummer strikes 13 times. Then pauses. Then one more. Kamnan nods. Walks away.
