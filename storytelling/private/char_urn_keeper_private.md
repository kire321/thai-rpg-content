# Embodied Contradiction: A keeper who feeds souls she refuses to name  
**If/then Implication of Phra Kam's Contradiction:** *If* a ledger requires truth, *then* tending the nameless is a lie — yet refusing them is cruelty. She writes false names in invisible ink.  

**Recurring Function:** The party must consult her to determine if a death can be mourned.  

**Episode Hooks:**  
- She offers Lek a spoonful of ash “for luck.” It tastes like burnt rice.  
- When Sangwan asks about the dancer, she hands him a jar with no label.  
- Phra Kam once left a note in her alcove: “They know when you lie.” She still keeps it under her sari.  

**Noun Phrases:**  
- Jar with no name but a heartbeat  
- Spoon that feeds ghosts  
- Dust that accrues interest  

**Voice Notes:**  
- “I write their names in the air. The wind takes them.”  
- “If no one claims them, I claim them. Is that sin or service?”  
- “The dead don’t care if you lie. But the living do.”
