### Dialectic  
A LIFE IS A CHART — (d) cities and reigns have charts too — a reign change is a sky change.  

### The revealed thing and why it is an if/then of the thesis  
**If** a life is bound by celestial alignment, **then** so is the state. The Water Blessing Ghat is not just for children—it is a *reign marker*. The water level at coronation is etched into stone. When the king weakens, the water recedes. When a name is given here, it is not just to the child, but to the era. The drum’s 13-minute rhythm is not arbitrary: it matches the average human breath cycle during prayer, syncing the body to the reign’s pulse. The institution reveals that legitimacy flows from water, breath, and time—not law.
