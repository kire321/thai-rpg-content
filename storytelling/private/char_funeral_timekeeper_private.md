### If/then implication of the recurring character's contradiction  
Kamnan refuses to change recorded times, even for mercy, because truth without authority is suicide.  
**Then** the Funeral Timekeeper *does* change them—but only in red-to-black, a ritual of correction that pretends the lie was always true. She embodies the same contradiction: she serves the ledger, but knows it lies. Her earplugs are not for silence—they are to avoid hearing the truth.  

### Recurring function  
The party must file death certificates for inheritance, land transfer, or legal exemption.  

### Hooks  
- She gives Lek two times for the same death: one for the district, one for the family. “Burn the red one,” she whispers.  
- Kamnan visits monthly. She hands him a slip with the *true* death times. He records them in his private ledger.  

### Noun phrases

2. **the lie that holds**  
3. **ink that remembers**
