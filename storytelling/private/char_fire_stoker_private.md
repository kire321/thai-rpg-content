## If/Then Implication of Recurring Character's Contradiction
Pom contains grief by writing and burning; the Fire Stoker contains it by cooking and covering.  
*Smaller instance of the same shape:* The woman who keeps the village fed also keeps its sorrow buried—her calm is performance, her slowness a lid. She never mourns aloud, but feeds ashes to the soil where flowers grow too well.

## Recurring Function
The party must retrieve items hidden in the hearth’s embers, request extended burning for symbolic closure, or learn whose grief caused a fire to flare unexpectedly.

## Episode Hooks
- She refuses to stir the coals for a week after a boy drowns. The hearth goes cold. Families grumble. Then she whispers to Lek: “He called my name twice before going under. I’m not ready to cook over that.”
- Pom brings her a small wrapped bundle. She drops it into the pit without opening it. Later, she says: “That one burned fast. Too dry.”
