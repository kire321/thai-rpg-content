### If/then implication of the recurring character's contradiction  
Kamnan believes the chart is absolute but knows every chart is a lie.  
**Then** the Rain Gauge Keeper must *see* the first drop to start the monsoon, even though he knows rain began upstream hours earlier. His practice is a smaller version of Kamnan’s: he enforces a truth he knows is false, because the record must hold.  

### Recurring function  
The party must obtain the *official* start of the monsoon to file land claims, register crops, or challenge tax assessments.  

### Hooks  
- The keeper refuses to log rain that fell while he was eating. He says, “I didn’t see it. It didn’t begin.”  
- Kamnan visits at dawn. The keeper nods, adjusts his stopwatch to match Kamnan’s chronometer. No words.  

### Noun phrases  
1. **rain without witness**  
2. **the first drop that wasn’t first**  
3. **time still burning**  

### Voice notes  
> “If I didn’t see it, it didn’t begin.”  
> “The sky doesn’t keep records. We do.”  
> “You can’t plant in truth. You plant in paper.”
