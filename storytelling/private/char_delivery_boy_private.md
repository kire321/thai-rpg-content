### If/Then Implication of the Recurring Character's Contradiction:  
*A man whose duty is to deliver truth, yet controls its arrival so it wounds less — just as Phum alters words, the boy alters timing.* Both serve truth by managing its impact. His silence mirrors Phum’s calculated pauses; his stillness, the same containment.

### Recurring Function:  
The party uses him to send sensitive messages — knowing he will choose *when* they appear, not just *if*. They also interrogate the order of incoming mail to infer village tensions.

### Episode Hooks:  
- He slips Pricha a sealed envelope that arrived for the District Officer — marked “after sunset.” When opened, it contains only a date and a dried lotus petal.  
- After Phum reads the forged remittance letter, the boy delays all school notices by one day. No explanation. The pattern stops only when Phum hands him a pair of mended spectacles.

### Noun Phrases:  
**arrival held breathless**  
**message gone leaning**  
**time paid in stillness**

### Voice Notes:  
- Answers questions with minimal nods or finger movements — thumb up/down, side-to-side. Only speaks when delivering confirmation: “Received,” “Released,” “Returned.”  
- Never refers to people by name — uses spatial markers: “the one who stands in shade,” “the one whose shadow crosses first.”  
- When pressured, repeats the last sentence spoken to him — tone unchanged, as if playback.
