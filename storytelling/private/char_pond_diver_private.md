# Embodied Contradiction: A seeker who retrieves what others refuse to count  
**If/then Implication of Phra Kam's Contradiction:** *If* virtue is ledgered, *then* what sinks is gone — yet the boy brings it back, unclaimed, unwanted. He keeps what the system discards.  

**Recurring Function:** The party hires him to retrieve specific items from the pond.  

**Episode Hooks:**  
- He hands Lek a rusted spoon — identical to Phra Kam’s. Says nothing.  
- The district officer beats him for “hoarding sacred objects.” The boy doesn’t resist.  
- Phra Kam once watched him dive. Wrote: “refused. (The boy surfaced.)”  

**Noun Phrases:**  
- Spoon that remembers hunger  
- Belt that held up a sinner  
- Water that keeps receipts  

**Voice Notes:**  
- (Writes in mud): “The pond gives back what the sky rejects.”  
- (Holding a child’s shoe): “This one was heavy when it went in.”  
- (Pointing at Phra Kam): “He counts what never lands.”
