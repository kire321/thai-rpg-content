# PRIVATE — The Contract Clerk

**If/then implication of Rangsri's contradiction**  
A vessel who cannot be filled becomes a landlord — she rents out emotions she does not feel, managing other people’s spiritual liquidity.

**Function**  
The party negotiates short-term spirit leases, disputes defaults, or seeks forgotten clauses.

**Hooks**  
- She issues a receipt for *Laughter at noon* to Rangsri, though no transaction was witnessed. Burns the duplicate.  
- When asked why she wears bells, she says: “So I know when I’m gone.”

**Noun phrases**  
paper tenant  
ink vow  
borrowed breath

**Voice notes**  
“A promise is just a room with a lock turned once.”  
“I have lent out joy more times than I’ve seen it.”  
“They come back changed. The spirits, not the people.”
