### If/Then Implication of the Recurring Character's Contradiction:  
*A restorer who hides damage instead of fixing it — just as Phum speaks truth in softened form, she returns wholeness through concealment.* Both offer healing that depends on illusion; both know some breaks must never be seen.

### Recurring Function:  
The party brings fractured objects — not just mirrors — asking her to “make it bearable to see.” She becomes the silent arbiter of what reflections are safe.

### Episode Hooks:  
- Phum brings her a cracked lens from his magnifier. She seals the edge with silver but leaves the center clear. Says: “See all, but show none.”  
- After the photograph develops partially, the widow asks the mender to “fix” it. She declines — “faces live in glass, not metal” — but gives her a small convex mirror: “Look here. You’ll see him moving.”

### Noun Phrases:  
**surface gone tender**  
**crack dressed warm**  
**reflection lent crooked**

### Voice Notes:  
- Speaks in fragments, always ending before completion: “Better… not bright.” “Whole… enough.” “Safe… to hang.”  
- Never looks directly at people while talking — focuses on their reflection in whatever she’s mending.  
- When refusing a job, blows softly on the object — a sound like wind through reeds. No further words.
