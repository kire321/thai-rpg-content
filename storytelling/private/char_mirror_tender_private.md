# Embodied Contradiction: A custodian who maintains a system he believes is hollow  
**If/then Implication of Phra Kam's Contradiction:** *If* merit is real, *then* the mirror is fraud — yet if it’s fraud, why do people weep before it? He polishes the glass to keep the lies smooth.  

**Recurring Function:** The party must gain access to the mirror’s hidden drawer — he alone holds the key.  

**Episode Hooks:**  
- He finds a ring in the silt — engraved with a name the temple denies. He returns it to the water.  
- He tells Lek: “You can’t donate what you don’t believe in. But you can pretend well enough.”  
- Phra Kam once sat beside him for three days. The mirror cracked. He still hasn’t repaired it.  

**Noun Phrases:**  
- Glass that drinks reflection  
- Funnel with a conscience  
- Shame that burns cleaner than gold  

**Voice Notes:**  
- “I’ve seen men give everything and still look hungry.”  
- “The rich drop gold and see saints. The poor drop rice and see ghosts.”  
- “I don’t believe in the mirror. But I believe in the tears.”
