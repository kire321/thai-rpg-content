# PRIVATE — The Laughing Well

**Dialectic**  
*Thesis implication:* If the soul is a guest, then joy is not indulgence but maintenance — a creaking door invites wind, but a singing hinge holds fast. Laughter here is structural, not celebratory: it tightens the bonds between body and inhabitant.

**Revealed thing**  
The well answers not prayers but punchlines. To draw water after drought, a joke must be told — not remembered, not repeated, but invented fresh. Those whose jokes fall flat find their buckets empty, their throats dry. The best jokers are widows and mute boys.

**Episode hooks**  
- Rangsri sits for three hours, humming into the stone. On the fourth, she tells a joke no one hears. The water rises one inch.  
- A girl drops a marble tied to a ribbon — her first laugh since fever. The well bubbles once. Her shadow walks home alone.

joy ledger
