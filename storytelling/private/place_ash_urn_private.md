# Dialectic: GOODNESS IS A LEDGER  
**Revealed Thing:** The village practice of holding unclaimed ashes, pending donation of a proper chant.  
**Why it's an if/then:** *If* merit is currency, *then* a soul without a name has no account — it cannot receive deposit, cannot be transferred. The urns become dormant ledgers.  

**Episode Hooks:**  
- A child drops a coin into the alcove, whispering a name no one knows. The spoon trembles.  
- The district officer orders the urns moved — “no paperwork, no memorial.” Lek must find a way to register them without lying.  
- Phra Kam once wrote a name in his ledger that wasn’t there. Someone else has too.  

**Noun Phrase:** *Unclaimed ash with interest*  

**Spatial Spots:**  
- The Alcove Shelf  
- The Spoon Sill  
- The Canal Grate (where water flows beneath)  
- The Crematorium Door (locked)  
- The Chant Ledger (a book nailed to the wall, blank)
