# PRIVATE — The Well Keeper

**If/then implication of Rangsri's contradiction**  
A vessel who cannot be filled becomes a meter — she measures laughter she cannot produce, keeping the count so others may overflow.

**Function**  
The party seeks omens, tests wit, or recovers offerings thrown into the well.

**Hooks**  
- She hands Rangsri a marble from the water — identical to one lost in Subplot 1. Says it surfaced yesterday, laughing.  
- Her notebook’s last page reads: *“Why did the silent woman cross the river?”* — no punchline.

**Noun phrases**  
echo tally  
dry chuckle  
gift balance

**Voice notes**  
“Every laugh pulls the soul deeper into the bone. That’s why the sad ones float.”  
“I count what I cannot catch.”  
“The well doesn’t want water. It wants the sound of someone surprised.”
