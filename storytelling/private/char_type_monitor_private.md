## If/Then Implication of Recurring Character's Contradiction
Pom fears editing distorts voice; the Type Monitor believes editing *is* voice.  
*Smaller instance of the same shape:* The woman who polices grammar to preserve dignity carries secret rage at being called “the old fuss.” Her restraint is ideology; her grief is that no one sees the violence in careless words.

## Recurring Function
The party must submit written notices, challenge altered texts, or recover phrases removed during cooling. She controls access to the press and interprets “cooling violations.”
