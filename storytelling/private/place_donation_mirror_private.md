# Dialectic: GOODNESS IS A LEDGER  
**Revealed Thing:** Anonymous giving as audit evasion.  
**Why it's an if/then:** *If* merit is countable, *then* unrecorded gifts leave no trace — no credit, no guilt. The mirror reflects only those who wish to be unseen.  

**Episode Hooks:**  
- A woman places a golden lotus on the tray — her husband died in prison. The mirror shows her younger self.  
- The temple accountant demands the funnel’s contents be measured. No one knows how to weigh waterlogged rice.  
- Phra Kam sits across the canal. He writes: “refused. (Again.)”  

**Noun Phrase:** *Offering that drowns its name*  

**Spatial Spots:**  
- The Floating Tray  
- The Mirror Frame  
- The Brass Funnel  
- The Silted Bank (where things wash up)  
- The Accountant’s Hut (onshore, barred)
