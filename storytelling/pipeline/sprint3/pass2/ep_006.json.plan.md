## STICKY SITUATION
The Dance Teacher Bua collapses in her studio, whispering a phrase in an old dialect. The District Officer declares the phrase seditious and bans the village’s upcoming Wai Khru performance, demanding a formal, neutral appeal be submitted before the ceremony or it will be canceled.
