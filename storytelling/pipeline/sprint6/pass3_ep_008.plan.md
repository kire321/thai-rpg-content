## STICKY SITUATION
A sealed, damp-stained envelope arrives at the River Post addressed to Kamnan Noi. The District Officer demands Lek open and translate it immediately, suspecting it contains a seditious chart from Bangkok. Kamnan refuses to take it.

### Act 2
PRESENT: Lek, Kamnan Noi, The District Officer
1. The three of them walk in silence to the River Post, the District Officer carrying the envelope.
2. At the post, the District Officer uses his penknife to slit the envelope's top, but does not pull the contents out.
3. He slides it across the low wooden platform to Lek.
4. Kamnan Noi stands apart, adjusting his brass compass against the sun's angle over the water.
5. Lek extracts a single sheet of official paper, densely printed with tables of numbers and astrological symbols.
6. SUBPLOT: At the bottom of the page, below a stamped seal, is a handwritten line in disappearing ink that has already begun to fade.
7. The District Officer demands a translation of the printed text.
8. Kamnan Noi speaks without turning: "It is a tide table for the Chao Phraya estuary. From the Bureau of Celestial Timekeeping."
9. The District Officer snatches the page, squinting at the symbols.

### Act 3
PRESENT: Lek, Kamnan Noi, The District Officer, The Salt Clerk
1. The District Officer, unsatisfied, marches them to the Salt Store, saying the light is better there.
2. Inside, the Salt Clerk looks up from her red-chalk ledger but says nothing.
3. The District Officer smooths the damp paper on the glass over the Salt Clerk's ledger.
4. He orders Lek to read the numbers column by column, aloud.
5. Lek begins reading latitudes, longitudes, and times of high water.
6. Kamnan Noi corrects a minute in one of the times Lek reads, his voice flat.
7. The District Officer interrupts, pointing to the faded handwritten line. "And that?"
8. Lek peers closer; only a few numerals are still visible.
9. THESIS "debt held in grain": Kamnan Noi takes a small bottle of clear liquid from his robe and offers it to the District Officer. "This will restore the ink. If you wish to see."

### Act 4
PRESENT: Lek, Kamnan Noi, The District Officer, The Monk, The Pawnshop Appraiser
1. They return to the veranda; the Monk and Pawnshop Appraiser are still there.
2. The District Officer hands the now-dry official page back to Kamnan Noi.
3. "File this with your other records," he says. "I will check the filing date."
4. Kamnan Noi takes the page and places it in his public ledger without looking at it.
5. He opens a different, smaller ledger and makes an entry with a sharp pencil.
6. The District Officer leaves, his watch still on the veranda floor where he left it.
7. The Pawnshop Appraiser picks up the watch, polishes it on his sleeve, and sets it gently next to Kamnan Noi.
8. Lek watches Kamnan Noi. Kamnan Noi does not touch the watch.
9. The Delivery Boy returns, placing a second, identical dry envelope on the veranda beside Lek.
10. Kamnan Noi looks at this new envelope, then at his compass, and makes another entry in his ledger.
