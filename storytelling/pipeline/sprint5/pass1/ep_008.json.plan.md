## STICKY SITUATION
A water-blessing for a new boat is scheduled for tomorrow morning at the river post. Kamnan Noi refuses to allow the naming of the boatman’s son during the ceremony, stating the boy’s birth hour is cursed. The boatman and his wife have already carved the name on the prow, and the district officer will be there to register it for the rice subsidy.
