## Act 1 — The Spirit-Renter’s Ledger
PLACE: The School Veranda
NARRATOR: The boy sits on the veranda steps, staring at the canal water and laughing a high, dry sound that carries no smile.
NARRATOR: The boy’s father stands behind him, a rolled paper in his fist, his eyes fixed on Lek.
NARRATOR: The Boatman poles his narrow boat past the veranda; the Monk walks along the canal path and pauses; the Fire Stoker crosses from the school’s kitchen shed, wiping soot from his hands.
The Father: [unrolls the paper on the school table] "The district officer demands a stamped receipt from the salt store by sundown, or the boy loses his place in the school ledger forever."
NARRATOR: Lek traces the boy’s thumbprint on the request; the ink is fresh, but the print is smudged.
NARRATOR: Rangsri knows the father's deadline; she places her lacquered box on the table beside the paper without opening it. [[tag_113]]
NARRATOR: Rangsri hums a single, low note; the boy stops laughing and turns his head toward the box.
NARRATOR: Rangsri opens her box; inside are seven small vials, one of which holds a faint, shimmering vapor.
The Father: [points to the vial] "That is the first payment. The spirit wants the next one by sundown."
Lek: [leans forward] "What is the second payment?"
Rangsri: [catches the question before it fades] "It is a game played without reason, and it must happen at the rice ledger." [[tag_029]]
