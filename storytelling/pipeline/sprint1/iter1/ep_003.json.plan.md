## STICKY SITUATION  
The widow arrives at the veranda with a folded death certificate, demanding the time of her husband’s passing be corrected. The district officer has set a noon deadline for land audits, and the current record bars her from inheritance.  

## OUTLINE  
### Act 1  
1. The widow slams a paper on the veranda rail; Lek sees the ink is smudged where someone scraped out the time.  
2. Kamnan Noi adjusts his compass, murmuring the exact minute the clock in the funeral parlor stopped.  
3. The Salt Clerk steps forward, barefoot, saying the widow traded salt for silence at dawn.  
4. Lek asks who recorded the death; the Funeral Timekeeper points to his own ears, then to the crossed-out red numbers behind the counter.  
5. The Pawnshop Appraiser leans in, whispering that the deed is co-held and the audit begins at noon.  
6. Kamnan Noi opens his ledger, taps the entry: "4:13 AM", written in iron gall ink.  
7. The widow says her husband drew breath at 4:07, and the clock in the parlor jumped twice before stopping.  
8. Lek picks up the certificate; the space for the time is torn at the corner.

### Act 2  
1. The boatman arrives, dripping; he carries a lantern with a cracked glass face, lit from below.  
2. The veranda clock strikes uneven; Kamnan Noi flinches, then notes the exact second it stuttered.  
3. Lek follows the boatman to the funeral parlor, where the coffin lid rests on sawhorses.  
4. Close look at the clock: the pendulum is bent, the hands frozen at 4:13.  
5. The Funeral Timekeeper covers his ears when Lek asks about the chime.  
6. The widow points to the floorboards near the bed—three dark stains in a line.  
7. The Pawnshop Appraiser stands in the doorway; his shadow hides the deed in his palm.  
8. Lek finds a chalk mark behind the counter: "4:07" in small script.  
9. DECISION — dilemma line: "Do you file what’s true or what’s written?" / [easy] Hand the sealed ledger to the Pawnshop Appraiser. PASS: "I have something to tell you." FAIL: "I don’t feel the same." / [medium] Read the chalk mark aloud. PASS: "I can barely hear you." FAIL: "That’s no problem." / [hard] Break the clock’s glass to check the mechanism. PASS: "I knew right away." FAIL: "It’s right here."  

### Act 3  
1. The district officer enters, boots loud; he stands in front of the widow, blocking the door.  
2. The Rain Gauge Keeper calls from the landing: rain started at 3:55 AM, not 4:00.  
3. Kamnan Noi steps forward, places his chronometer on the counter—time still burning.  
4. The widow burns the certificate in the lantern; the flame dips at 4:07.  
5. The Pawnshop Appraiser grabs the deed; the ink runs where his fingers sweat.  
6. Lek sees the same time—4:07—scratched inside the coffin rim.  
7. The boatman says the river rose at 4:08, just before dawn.  
8. DECISION — dilemma line: "Which record do you submit to the school?" / [easy] Submit the monk’s version. PASS: "It’s the best way." FAIL: "I’m not finished yet." / [medium] Submit both times with a note. PASS: "Now I know." FAIL: "Don’t throw it away!" / [hard] Refuse to file until the district officer leaves. PASS: "I believe you can do it." FAIL: "Are you finished yet?"  

### Act 4  
1. The veranda is empty except for Kamnan Noi, writing in his sealed ledger.  
2. Lek returns, hands the school ledger to the monk.  
3. The Fire Stoker arrives, saying the widow’s house caught at 3:58 AM.  
4. Kamnan Noi closes his book, hands Lek a key.  
5. The sky turns paper-white as the sun clears the trees.  
6. Lek pockets the key; the boatman nods from the landing.  
7. The Monk says the chart is blind; Kamnan Noi does not answer.  
8. The veranda clock ticks once, then stops.
