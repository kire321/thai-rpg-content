## Act 1 — The Blank Plate
PLACE: place_veranda
NARRATOR: The envelope from Bangkok lies on the veranda floorboards between Lek’s feet and Phum’s stool.
NARRATOR: The Pond Diver picks it up, turns it over, and places it on Lek’s open ledger.
NARRATOR: The print bureau’s seal is intact, but the paper around it is soft from handling.
Lek: "Do you expect correspondence?"
NARRATOR: Phum cleans his lens on his sleeve and looks at the envelope but does not touch it.
Phum: "I want to know what they are asking for, and it will cost me a trip to the letter-writer."
NARRATOR: He says he does not.
NARRATOR: The Monk arrives from the temple hall, holding a palm-leaf manuscript.
The Monk: "Let us open it."
NARRATOR: Lek breaks the seal.
The Pond Diver: "โทรกลับหาพี่ด้วยนะ."
NARRATOR: Lek gives a call to him, but receives no answer. [[tag_020]]
NARRATOR: Inside is a photographic plate, wrapped in black paper.
NARRATOR: The plate is smooth and blank.
NARRATOR: No letter accompanies it.
NARRATOR: A separate slip of paper bears a date: the next mail boat’s arrival, two days hence.
The Monk: "The plate is blank, but the bureau demands a reply. How do we proceed?"
The Pond Diver: "ไม่เหนื่อยเหรอ?"
NARRATOR: The question hangs in the humid air as Phum looks tired. [[tag_284]]

## Act 2 — The Fixed Plate
PLACE: place_veranda
NARRATOR: The District Officer arrives at the veranda, holding a folded government circular.
NARRATOR: He stands between Lek and the canal steps.
The District Officer: "Has any reply been prepared for the bureau?"
NARRATOR: Phum picks up the blank plate and holds it to the light.
Phum: "The plate is not blank; it has been fixed."
Phum: "I need a developing tray."
The District Officer: "The bureau requires an answer about the registry of plates from the fifth reign."
NARRATOR: Phum places the plate back into its black paper.
Phum: "I will need to visit the letter-writer’s landing to prepare a reply."
NARRATOR: Lek closes the ledger over the envelope.
The District Officer: "I will accompany you to ensure the reply is timely."
NARRATOR: Phum gathers his mending tools into a cloth bundle.
NARRATOR: The three of them walk down to the canal.
The District Officer: "ชอบสุดๆ."
NARRATOR: Lek nods, his expression unreadable, showing no love for the comment. [[tag_317]]
The District Officer: "The District Officer insists on accompanying us. How do we manage his oversight?"
NARRATOR: Phum squints at the path.
Phum: "ผมสายตาไม่ดี."
NARRATOR: The District Officer offers no help, ignoring Phum's poor vision. [[tag_141]]

## Act 3 — The Registry Number
PLACE: place_letter_writers_landing
NARRATOR: At the letter-writer’s landing, the old man has already set out his inkstone.
NARRATOR: The District Officer stands by the pigeonhole rack.
Phum: "A clean sheet of paper and the use of your darkest ink."
NARRATOR: He does not write.
NARRATOR: Instead, he unwraps the plate again and lays it flat on the table.
Phum: "Lek, the school’s magnifying glass."
NARRATOR: Lek hands it to him.
NARRATOR: Phum tilts the plate in the sun.
NARRATOR: He traces his finger over a corner of the glass.
Phum: "There is a registry number etched along the edge, visible only at an angle."
NARRATOR: The District Officer leans in to look.
NARRATOR: Phum recites the number from memory.
Phum: "Transcribe this exactly onto the clean paper."
NARRATOR: The letter-writer’s hand shakes as he writes.
Phum: "The plate itself is the only suitable reply."
NARRATOR: He rewraps it and places it back into the original envelope.
NARRATOR: He seals the envelope with a drop of wax from the letter-writer’s candle.
NARRATOR: He hands the sealed envelope to the District Officer.
Phum: "ต้องดูให้ได้นะ."
NARRATOR: Lek meets his gaze, determined to see what Phum has found. [[tag_219]]
Phum: "The registry number is found, but how do we ensure the reply reaches the bureau correctly?"
NARRATOR: The District Officer steps closer.
The District Officer: "ผมได้กลิ่นน้ำหอม."
NARRATOR: The scent is faint and floral, a trace of perfume in the air. [[tag_153]]

## Act 4 — The Incomplete Record
PLACE: place_veranda
NARRATOR: Back on the veranda, the District Officer is gone.
NARRATOR: The mail boat horn sounds from down the canal.
NARRATOR: Phum sits back on his stool and picks up a pair of broken spectacles.
NARRATOR: He begins winding brass wire around a hinge.
NARRATOR: Lek opens the ledger to a fresh page.
NARRATOR: He writes the date and the bureau’s name.
NARRATOR: He writes the registry number Phum recited.
NARRATOR: He leaves the rest of the line blank.
NARRATOR: The Pond Diver brings two cups of tea.
NARRATOR: Phum accepts one but does not drink.
NARRATOR: He looks at the canal where the boat departed.
NARRATOR: He sets the teacup down on the floorboard beside his stool, untouched.
The Pond Diver: "ยังใช้ได้อยู่."
NARRATOR: Phum does not reach for it, ignoring that it can still be of use. [[tag_339]]
The Pond Diver: "The reply is sent, but the ledger entry is incomplete. How do we close the record?"
NARRATOR: Lek closes the ledger.
Lek: "มีเรื่องจะบอก."
NARRATOR: The veranda falls silent, waiting for what he will tell them. [[tag_325]]
