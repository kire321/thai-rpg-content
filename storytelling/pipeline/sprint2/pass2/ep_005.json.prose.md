## Act 1 — The Notice
PLACE: The School Veranda
NARRATOR: Lek is the teacher of the veranda school, kneeling on the worn floorboards with the school's tin document box open before him, while The District Officer stands straight in crisp uniform, tapping a bamboo pointer against a freshly pasted notice on the pillar.
NARRATOR: Phra Kam sits perfectly still in faded robes by the pond's edge with his back to the veranda, a small black notebook beside him, and Wan stands in the shadow of the doorway with a cloth in her hands, watching silently.
NARRATOR: The Boatman is a lean man in loose clothes, sitting on the canal bank a short distance away, mending a fishing net in his lap.
The District Officer: "Produce the monk's temple papers within three days, or the fine will be levied on this school."
The Boatman: "ไม่ไปไหนเหรอ"
NARRATOR: Lek looks up from the box and shakes his head, his search stalled. [[tag_283]]
Wan: "ชอบสุดๆ"
NARRATOR: Lek glances at Phra Kam's serene stillness, then turns back to face The District Officer. [[tag_317]]
NARRATOR: The District Officer’s notice is pasted over a crack in the veranda pillar, its red stamp bleeding into the old wood.
NARRATOR: Phra Kam opens his small black notebook and writes a single word with a charcoal pencil, then closes it.

## Act 2 — The Pawnshop
PLACE: The Pawnshop
NARRATOR: The Pawnshop Appraiser, a stout man in a sleeveless shirt, stands on the pawnshop landing with a small scale weighing a bundle of gold tied with red string, while Lek walks with Phra Kam along the canal toward the pawnshop.
NARRATOR: The Boatman poles his boat alongside them, keeping pace.
Lek: "Do you have any document from your old temple?"
NARRATOR: Phra Kam shakes his head once.
The Pawnshop Appraiser: "มีขายที่"
NARRATOR: Lek scans the shelves, looking for anything that could serve as proof. [[tag_050]]
NARRATOR: At the pawnshop landing, The Pawnshop Appraiser is weighing a bundle of red-stringed gold.
The Boatman: "พูดจริงนะ"
NARRATOR: Lek nods firmly, his determined expression unchanged, and approaches the appraiser's counter. [[tag_124]]
Lek: "Have you ever taken a monk’s bowl or robe as pledge?"
NARRATOR: The Pawnshop Appraiser sets down the scale and looks at Lek with a blank expression.
The Pawnshop Appraiser: "ไม่เคย"

## Act 3 — The Rice Ledger
PLACE: The Rice Ledger
NARRATOR: The Tax Clerk stamps a ledger page forcefully at a high wooden counter, while The Grain Clerk stacks wooden tallies in the corner, his sleeves rolled up.
NARRATOR: Inside the office, the air is thick with mildew and ink.
NARRATOR: The Tax Clerk's fingers are stained with ink.
NARRATOR: Lek approaches the counter and asks about registration for a monk without a temple.
The Grain Clerk: "อยู่จังหวัดอะไร"
NARRATOR: Lek hesitates, unable to name a province for Phra Kam. [[tag_126]]
NARRATOR: The Grain Clerk says monks are registered by the Sangha, not the tax office, and the Tax Clerk adds that for a fine, a name can be entered into the residence book, as he slides the heavy ledger toward Lek, open to a column of thumbprints, and The District Officer enters and stands behind him.
The Tax Clerk: "ทุกคนรู้จักมัน"
NARRATOR: The District Officer nods in agreement with the clerk, acknowledging the regulation. [[tag_113]]
The District Officer: "Have you found the papers?"
The District Officer: "If there is no paper, there is no person for the ledger."

## Act 4 — The Declaration
PLACE: The School Veranda
NARRATOR: Back on the veranda, the afternoon light slants across the pond, and The District Officer waits with his pointer on his shoulder, while the Boatman ties his boat to the veranda post and Lek places the school’s tin box on the floor between them.
NARRATOR: He takes out a blank sheet and his pen, writes a line, and reads it aloud: he declares Phra Kam a ward of the veranda.
Wan: "มีอะไรเข้าตา"
NARRATOR: Lek pauses his reading and looks at her, blinking in the slanting light, then continues. [[tag_203]]
NARRATOR: Lek says the veranda is a place of teaching, and a teacher may host a guest, and The District Officer laughs, asking if a guest eats from the school's rice, then Phra Kam takes the rusted spoon from his robe and holds it flat on his palm.
The Boatman: "ขอไม้จิ้มฟันหน่อย"
NARRATOR: The District Officer shakes his head at the spoon, turns, and walks down the steps without another word. [[tag_060]]
NARRATOR: Phra Kam looks at the spoon in his hand and puts it back into his robe.
NARRATOR: The veranda is quiet, except for the sound of the Boatman adjusting his net.
NARRATOR: Lek watches the District Officer's boat drift away.
