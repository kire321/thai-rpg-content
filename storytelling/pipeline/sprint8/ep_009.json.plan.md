## STICKY SITUATION
The monthly silver letter from Wan’s supposed sister did not arrive in Bangkok. Their mother has written to the veranda asking why the money stopped, and she is coming down the canal by boat to see her daughter. The Dance Teacher, who knows the sister is dead, demands that Wan write the truth to their mother now.
