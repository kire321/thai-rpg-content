# World Building Plan

## Core Concept: The Resonance

The world is called **Chantara** (from Thai: จันทรา, meaning "moonlady," evoking both the sky-above and the feminine principle of receptivity).

### The Lattice

Millennia ago, a catastrophic event called the **Silencing** shattered the world's surface into an impassable crystalline lattice that reacts to sound. The lattice is not merely a terrain feature; it is a vast, semi-aware membrane of resonant crystal that covers the entire planet. Different frequencies cause different reactions:
- Certain tones create safe pathways through the crystal
- Others cause lethal crystalline growths to erupt
- Some frequencies make the lattice transparent, revealing the world beneath
- The lattice "remembers" sounds that have been played near it, creating echoes and residual safe zones

### The Skycities

Human civilization survives on **floating archipelagos** - massive islands of accumulated organic matter, ancient debris, and mineral deposits that drift on thermals above the lattice. Each skycity is tethered by great **resonance anchors** - crystalline spires that extend down into the lattice and hold the city in place through sustained harmonic vibration.

Skycities vary wildly in character:
- **Khrueang**: The largest skycity, a trade hub built from the hulls of a thousand ancient resonance ships
- **Phrao**: A monastery-city where monks study the lattice's song and catalog safe frequencies
- **Tha Khwae**: A young, rebellious city of scavengers who harvest lattice fragments for technology
- **Mae Rim**: An agricultural city where floating gardens produce crops adapted to thin air and cold

### The Resonance Ships

The only way to travel between skycities is by **resonance ship** - vessels that use specialized "singing engines" to vibrate at frequencies that part the lattice below them, creating temporary safe channels. These ships are:
- Piloted by **Singers** who know the ancient tonal routes
- Fueled by **crystal cores** harvested from the lattice
- Highly dangerous, as lattice conditions shift unpredictably

### Society and Culture

- **The Tonal Orders**: Guilds of Singers who guard the secret routes between cities. They speak in specialized tonal dialects that can also manipulate the lattice in emergencies.
- **The Listeners**: A religious order that believes the lattice is trying to communicate and spends their lives recording and analyzing its "natural" sounds.
- **Scavengers**: Outcasts who live on the lattice's surface in fortified mobile platforms, harvesting crystal and searching for pre-Silencing artifacts.
- **The Groundless**: A political movement that wants to find a permanent way to live on the lattice surface rather than in the sky.

### Why This World Is Non-Obvious

- No magic system in the traditional sense; the lattice follows acoustic physics, just exaggerated to mythic proportions
- No evil dark lord; the primary antagonist is the lattice itself and the logistical impossibility of survival
- The "dungeon" equivalent is navigating the lattice's surface, where every step requires the right sound
- Thai language learning fits naturally: tones and vowel lengths literally determine survival
- The world is vertical rather than horizontal, creating a unique spatial dynamic
