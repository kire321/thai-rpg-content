# Characters Plan

## Party Members

### Chanida (Singer-in-Training)
A young woman from Khrueang who possesses an undocumented tonal range allowing her to hear lattice frequencies others cannot. She's impatient with the Tonal Orders' secrecy and believes resonance knowledge should be shared freely. Her rebelliousness often puts her at odds with authority, but her unique gift makes her indispensable for navigating uncharted lattice regions.

### Pichit (Former Listener Monk)
A contemplative man from the monastery-city Phrao who left after a spiritual crisis questioning whether the lattice is truly communicating or merely reacting. He carries a resonant staff carved from a single crystal shard that allows short-distance communication through the lattice itself. His measured, philosophical approach balances the party's more impulsive members.

### Malee (Scavenger Engineer)
A pragmatic survival expert who grew up on mobile platforms on the lattice surface. She has a prosthetic leg made from harvested crystal core and can repair any resonance engine with scavenged parts. Deeply suspicious of skycity "softness," she provides the practical knowledge needed for surface expeditions.

### Arthit (Former Ship Captain)
A middle-aged man who captained resonance ships for twenty years before losing his vessel and crew to a catastrophic lattice surge. He carries maps of forgotten, dangerous routes tattooed on his skin in ancient tonal notation. Haunted by survivor's guilt, he seeks redemption by helping others navigate where official routes fail.

## NPC Types

### Skycity Resident
Ordinary people living on the floating archipelagos - farmers, craftspeople, children playing near anchor edges. They know little of the lattice beyond what the Tonal Orders tell them.

### Sky Pirate
Bandits who operate modified resonance ships, attacking trade vessels between skycities. They know secret frequencies that can destabilize another ship's safe channel.

### Trade Negotiator
Merchants who broker deals between isolated skycities, often traveling with Singer escorts. They carry news, letters, and rare goods from city to city.

### Listener Monk
Members of the religious order studying the lattice's "natural" sounds, seeking to decode what they believe is the lattice's attempt to communicate with humanity.

### Surface Scavenger
Hardy individuals living on fortified mobile platforms on the lattice surface, harvesting crystal fragments and searching for pre-Silencing artifacts.

## Narrator / Game Master

### The Chronicler
An unnamed observer who records the party's journey, providing context, exposition, and occasional commentary on the ethical implications of their choices.

## Staging Recurring NPCs

The staging expansion adds named contacts with a repeatable role and a clear
relationship to the lattice. They are not party members; an episode may use
one as a featured contact and another as local support.

- **Kanya — Anchor Keeper:** hears failing tethers as changes in a platform's pulse and records warnings the Orders dismiss.
- **Wichai — Resonance Diver:** descends temporary channels for crystal cores and returns with unsettling memories of the lattice.
- **Maliwan — Surface Medic:** runs a mobile clinic and teaches skycity patients to recognize resonance sickness.
- **Jintana — Order Clerk:** copies route permits while leaking censored frequencies to people the Orders would silence.
- **Sakchai — Pirate Navigator:** reads storms from ship rails and searches for a way out of an old raiding debt.
- **Pailin — Crystal Artisan:** makes bowls that preserve a spoken tone and hides a workshop for Groundless families.
- **Rung — Groundless Organizer:** builds surface shelters and advocates for permanent settlement without gambling with volunteers' lives.
- **Nop — Archive Conservator:** repairs route tablets and tracks the deliberate gaps in pre-Silencing records.
- **Chaiyo — Storm Cartographer:** maps wind shear with singing glass kites and trades forecasts for uncensored charts.
- **Lalida — Skybridge Courier:** moves letters and endangered people through the private entrances of cable terminals.
